## Title

Sensorium — A Distributed Human-Agent Field Laboratory with WebMCP

## Description

Sensorium gives AI agents a human-authorized way to investigate physical environments. People provide presence, context, and sensor permission; agents provide protocols, coordination, evidence validation, comparison, and interpretation.

This demo follows Field Desk Atlas: a temporary focus expedition connecting a real browser capture in Belém with a clearly labeled deterministic station in São Paulo. Camera and microphone capture only starts after a person acts, raw media stays on-device, and every human or agent action returns to one visible evidence ledger.

Live app: https://sensorium-devsnorte.fly.dev/
Open-source repository: https://github.com/iagocavalcante/sensorium
Remote MCP endpoint: https://sensorium-devsnorte.fly.dev/mcp
