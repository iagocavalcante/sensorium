## Design Context

### Users
Sensorium is for curious people, citizen scientists, knowledge workers, and WebMCP Challenge judges who want to investigate how a physical environment affects a human outcome. A person supplies movement, perception, and permission; their browser agent supplies experimental structure, comparison, and interpretation.

### Brand Personality
Tactile, incisive, alive. The product should create curiosity and confidence while making measurement feel approachable rather than clinical or intimidating.

### Aesthetic Direction
A warm editorial field notebook crossed with a precise scientific instrument. Light-first paper neutrals, deep ink, mineral green, and a restrained vermilion signal color. Typography should feel authored and observational, with an expressive serif display face and a crisp humanist sans-serif body. Avoid generic AI dashboards, neon-on-black futurism, glassmorphism, excessive cards, and decorative charts that do not carry evidence.

### Design Principles
1. Make every agent action visible in the same evidence board the person sees.
2. Treat permission and physical action as part of the experience, never friction to conceal.
3. Prefer one memorable instrument view over a grid of generic metrics.
4. Let empty states teach the investigation loop: ask, observe, intervene, verify.
5. Maintain WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
